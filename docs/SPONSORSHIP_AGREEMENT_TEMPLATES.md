## Master Sponsorship Agreement

This Sponsorship and Marketing Partnership Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of `{{agreement_date}}` by and between Seattle Desi TV (“SDTV”), a Washington nonprofit organization recognized as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3), and `{{sponsor_legal_name}}` (“Sponsor”).

### 1. Partnership purpose

SDTV will provide the community visibility and promotional services described in Schedule A. Sponsor’s support helps SDTV advance community storytelling, cultural programming, youth engagement, and public-interest media. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, or endorsement of Sponsor’s products or services.

### 2. Term

The term begins `{{start_date}}` and ends `{{end_date}}`. Deliverables are scheduled cooperatively and must be completed within the term unless both parties agree otherwise in writing.

### 3. Fees and discount

Package: `{{package_name}}`. Standard price: `{{base_price}}`. Discount: `{{discount_description}}` (`{{discount_amount}}`). Final agreement amount: `{{final_amount}}`. This amount is fixed when the Agreement is accepted unless both parties approve a written amendment.

### 4. Payment schedule

Sponsor will pay according to Schedule B. Zelle payments must be sent to **info@seattledesitv.com** with reference `{{agreement_reference}}`. Sponsor will upload confirmation for each installment. An upload records payment as submitted; SDTV marks it complete only after verification. Sponsor remains responsible for all scheduled amounts unless a written amendment states otherwise.

### 5. Sponsor materials and approvals

Sponsor will timely provide accurate logos, brand guidelines, offers, claims, links, media, releases, and authorized spokesperson access. Sponsor represents that it has the right to provide and authorize SDTV to use those materials. Sponsor is responsible for the legality and accuracy of its advertising claims.

### 6. Production and revisions

SDTV controls its editorial and production methods while reasonably following the approved brief. `{{included_revision_rounds}}` reasonable revision rounds are included for sponsor-created deliverables. New concepts, reshoots, late scope changes, or revisions beyond the included rounds may require additional fees and scheduling.

### 7. Scheduling and performance

Dates are coordinated based on SDTV programming, production capacity, platform availability, and Sponsor responsiveness. Platform algorithms, audience response, sales, impressions, and business results are not guaranteed. Equivalent placement or rescheduling may be used when a channel, event, or production becomes unavailable.

### 8. Brand and content license

Sponsor grants SDTV a nonexclusive, royalty-free license during the term to use Sponsor’s approved name, logo, marks, and supplied materials solely to perform and promote this Agreement. SDTV retains ownership of content it creates unless a written addendum states otherwise. Sponsor may share SDTV’s published links with attribution but may not materially edit, resell, or commercially redistribute SDTV-created content without permission.

### 9. Cancellation and termination

Either party may terminate for a material breach not cured within `{{cure_period_days}}` days after written notice. SDTV may immediately suspend content that presents legal, safety, reputational, payment, or platform-policy concerns. `{{cancellation_terms}}` Rights and payment obligations accrued before termination survive.

### 10. Taxes and nonprofit disclosure

This Agreement provides promotional benefits in exchange for payment. No representation is made that any portion is tax-deductible. Sponsor should obtain independent tax advice; SDTV will separately identify any charitable contribution only when appropriate.

### 11. General terms

This Agreement and its schedules are the complete agreement and may be amended only in writing. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond reasonable control. Washington law governs. Electronic acceptance and signatures have the same effect as originals.
